Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Welcome to katekness.com. Your privacy matters to me.

This Privacy Policy explains how I collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you visit https://katekness.com, contact me, join my list, or explore my work.

This website is the central home for my professional work, including strategy and consulting, writing and books, and digital projects.

1. Information I collect

I collect personal information you choose to share with me, such as when you use the contact form or sign up for my list. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • What your message is about, and the message itself
  • How you found the form (for example, the website or an event)
  • Anything else you choose to share

The website and its service providers may also process limited technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and basic usage data.

I don't ask for sensitive personal information through the website.

2. How I use your information

  • To respond to questions, inquiries, and messages.
  • To talk about potential projects, collaborations, or professional opportunities.
  • To send information you've asked for, including my newsletter if you've signed up.
  • To keep appropriate records of professional communications.
  • To keep the website working, secure, and easy to use.
  • To meet legal obligations.

I do not sell your personal information.

Sending a contact message does not sign you up for my newsletter.

3. Contact form and newsletter

The contact form and the Girl, Listen newsletter signup are powered by Tally. What you submit is stored in my Tally account so I can read it and respond.

If you join my list, I'll use your email to send occasional updates about the book, events, and my writing. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe, or you can email me and I'll remove you.

I may use an email newsletter service to send those updates. If I do, your name and email will be stored there for that purpose only.

4. Third-party services

I use a small number of services to run this website and stay in touch:

  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website.
  • Tally: contact and signup forms.
  • Google Fonts: the fonts on this site, which your browser loads from Google.
  • HubSpot: contact management, if connected.
  • Email services: receiving and responding to messages.

These providers process information as needed to provide their services, under their own privacy policies.

5. External links

This website links to other websites and platforms, including:

  • Clarity Forward and related professional services.
  • Amazon and Barnes & Noble for buying my book.
  • Hello, Pixe! and other digital projects.
  • Instagram and LinkedIn.

Those sites run independently and have their own privacy policies, cookies, and data practices. I'm not responsible for them, so please review their policies before sharing personal information.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website doesn't use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. GitHub Pages may keep basic server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. The embedded Tally forms may use cookies or similar storage to work properly.

If I add analytics or other tracking tools, I'll update this policy and provide any notices or consent options the law requires.

7. How I protect your information

I take reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. Information may be processed or stored by third-party platforms that keep their own security measures.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please don't send passwords, financial account details, medical records, or other highly sensitive information through the contact form.

8. Keeping your information

I keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, keep relevant professional records, send emails you've asked for, or meet legal obligations.

You may ask me to delete your personal information, subject to legal and recordkeeping requirements.

9. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information. You can contact me to ask for:

  • Access to the personal information I have about you.
  • Correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion of your personal information, where applicable.
  • Information about how your personal information is used.
  • Removal from my newsletter or other optional emails.

I'll review and respond to privacy requests as the law requires.

10. Children's privacy

This website is a professional and personal portfolio and isn't directed to children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through katekness.com.

Some digital projects I link to are designed for younger audiences. Those projects run separately and may have their own privacy notices.

If I learn that personal information was collected from a child under 13 without appropriate permission, I'll take reasonable steps to delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy to reflect changes to my website, services, technology, or legal requirements. Updates will be posted here with a new effective date.
