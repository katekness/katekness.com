Girl, Listen

I write for people finding their way through hard seasons, reconnecting with themselves, and taking one honest next step.

My first book, Girl, Listen: Your Fascia Needs to Talk to You, explores the relationship between the body, emotions, and lived experience, told in fascia’s own (slightly sassy) voice.

Fun fact: fascia is literally your connective tissue. Of course the dot‑connector wrote a book about it.
