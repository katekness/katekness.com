About the author

Hi, I’m Kate.

Kate Kness is a writer based in Arizona who explores the relationship between the body and lived experience. She wrote this book because she wished someone had handed it to her sooner.

Book clubs, events, podcasts and interviews, or just to tell me what your fascia has been saying, I’d love to hear from you.
